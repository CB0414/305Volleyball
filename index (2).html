Our Five Pillars

1. Opportunity
We create access for every athlete, no matter their experience level. Whether you're brand new to the game or looking to refine advanced skills, 305 Volleyball gives you the chance to discover volleyball in a welcoming space.
2. Growth
Improvement never stops. We focus on developing technical skills, confidence, and character so athletes leave every session better than they arrived — both on the court and in life.
3. Community
Volleyball is better together. We build meaningful connections between athletes, coaches, and families, creating a supportive environment where everyone belongs and lifts each other up.
4. Passion
We foster a genuine love for the game that lasts beyond any single clinic or open gym. Our goal is for athletes to fall in love with volleyball and carry that passion with them.
5. Excellence
Excellence means giving your best effort and holding yourself to a high personal standard, whatever your current level. We celebrate mastery of the basics just as much as advanced play.

Our Philosophy

Every athlete has a different journey. We create the opportunity to discover the game, develop a love for it, build meaningful connections and pursue whatever level of success they envision.

305 Volleyball is a Staten Island-based volleyball training program focused on developing skills, building confidence, and fostering a love for the game in a positive, competitive, and supportive environment. We provide high-quality training opportunities year-round so athletes can continue improving both in and out of season.
